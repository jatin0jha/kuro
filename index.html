Welcome to Artistory

A calm place to browse and read from our shared bookshelf

What is Artistory?

Artistory is a reading catalog: every title links to its PDF so you can open it in a new tab and read at your own pace. Search by title or description, pick a cover from the grid, or start from the featured pick on the home page.

For readers

The full library loads from our live catalog. Your last opened book is remembered on this device so the featured section and side panel stay in sync while you explore.

For contributors

Have a book to share? Use Add book in the navigation to submit details through our Google Form. We review submissions before they appear in the catalog.

Get in touch

Questions, broken links, or feedback? Visit Contact in the menu and send us a message—we read every note.
